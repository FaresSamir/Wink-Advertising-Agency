TAP

BRIEF: Tap Café is a well-known spot in Tanta with over 6 years of presence. The café faced challenges on social media including lack of content variety, minimal use of reels, and limited engagement from the target audience. SOLUTION: We created a dynamic content strategy focused on trending topics, modern ideas, and creative ways to showcase menu items. We emphasized audience interaction by producing engaging social media content, covering live events with instant stories, and designing interactive campaigns for seasonal occasions like Eid. RESULTS: Increased social media engagement and interactions across platforms. Established Tap Café as a trendy, youth-friendly brand with distinctive content presentation. Boosted audience participation during events and strengthened brand loyalty through real-time coverage and creative storytelling.

PROFIT

BRIEF: Profit Gym is one of the largest fitness centers in Tanta and Mansoura. With the opening of its new branch in Tanta, the goal was to enter the market strongly as a premium gym offering a wide range of services, including fitness, spa, and wellness. SOLUTION: From day one of the new branch launch, we developed a powerful brand identity and positioned Profit Gym as the leading premium fitness destination. Our strategy included comprehensive content creation, innovative campaigns, and full social media management to showcase its unique services and facilities. RESULTS: Established Profit Gym as the market leader in Tanta’s fitness industry. Created a strong premium brand image that differentiates it from competitors. Achieved rapid growth, high engagement, and trust among the target audience.

RASHAT

BRIEF: Rashat is a Saudi fragrance brand offering a wide collection of personal perfumes and home mists. The brand aims to blend elegance with everyday use, making fragrances an essential part of style and lifestyle. SOLUTION: We established a strong presence on social media platforms to build brand awareness and increase engagement. The strategy focused on creating premium Arabic content in a unique tone of voice, blending modern visuals with culturally relevant messaging. In addition, we launched seasonal campaigns and promotional offers to drive direct sales through the website. RESULTS: Built a consistent and recognizable brand identity across social media. Increased customer engagement with creative visuals, bilingual content, and targeted campaigns. Launched successful promotional campaigns (e.g., “Buy 1 Get 1 Free” summer offer) that boosted online sales. Enhanced Rashat’s positioning as a premium fragrance brand in the Saudi market.

BADAWI

BRIEF: Dr. Moustafa Badawi is a renowned plastic surgeon specializing in body contouring surgeries. Before our collaboration, he had no presence on social media despite targeting a high-end audience (Class A) in both Egypt and the Gulf region. SOLUTION: We built his personal brand from the ground up by developing a strong visual identity and establishing a premium positioning strategy. We worked on all layers of personal branding, creating high-end content to highlight his expertise and surgical results. In addition, we executed offline marketing campaigns and managed his presence across all social media platforms to ensure maximum exposure and consistency. RESULTS: Created a distinguished personal brand that reflects professionalism and trust. Successfully reached and engaged Class A clients in Egypt and the Gulf. Positioned Dr. Moustafa Badawi as a top choice for body contouring surgeries through consistent, high-quality branding, online and offline campaigns.

PRICELESS PETS

BRIEF: Priceless Pets, a veterinary clinic and pet store in Jeddah, aimed to increase brand awareness, boost in-clinic visits, and grow online sales. The challenge was to build a strong brand presence on social media, highlight their wide range of services (medical, grooming, boarding, mobile clinic), and position the store as a trusted destination for pet products. SOLUTION: Developed a full content strategy focusing on branding, awareness, sales, and engagement. Worked with platform-specific content plans: Instagram (branding & engagement), TikTok (educational & awareness videos), and Snapchat (daily stories & local reach). Created Instagram grids, reels, and carousels showcasing services, seasonal offers, and pet care tips. Launched campaigns for home spa, and boarding services. Highlighted trusted international brands in the store (Royal Canin, Hill’s, Applaws, etc.). Implemented Google optimization (updated business data, encouraged reviews, ran ads). Designed special offers, bundles, and loyalty program to increase sales and repeat visits. RESULTS: Grew visibility on Google ,The clinic ranked in the Top 3 on Google search for key services, gained more customer reviews with higher ratings. Built a more professional and engaging Instagram identity. Increased customer interaction through educational and storytelling content. Boosted sales with exclusive offers, bundles, and seasonal campaigns. Strengthened brand positioning as a trusted and modern pet care destination in Jeddah
